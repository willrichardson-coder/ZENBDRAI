# Zendesk BDR manager one-on-one brief

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md).

Resolve exact rep, period, prior commitments, current outcomes, pipeline/meeting evidence, workflow artifacts, and known blockers. Separate system/data friction, skill development, business outcomes, confidence, and manager support.

Prepare a short agenda that begins with the rep's view, inspects one business decision and one development competency, checks prior commitments, and ends with no more than three concrete agreements. Use call evidence only when Gong or an authoritative transcript is read. Do not surprise the rep with conclusions inferred from weak proxies.

Return context, wins, evidence and questions, blocker decisions, development focus, rep commitments, manager commitments, and next check-in evidence. State that no message, calendar, or performance record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
