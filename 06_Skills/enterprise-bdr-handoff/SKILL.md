# Enterprise Bdr Handoff

Use after a prospect replies, accepts a meeting, confirms a problem, or requires seller ownership.

## Qualification standard

Do not qualify a handoff based only on seniority, positive sentiment, or calendar acceptance.

Capture:

- Account trigger or operating pressure
- Verified evidence and source links
- Prospect's stated issue or response
- Business impact, metric, or consequence if known
- Current environment or incumbent, only if verified
- Stakeholders and decision role
- Relevant Zendesk capability and why it fits
- Open discovery questions
- Agreed next step, owner, and date

## Output

Produce a concise handoff with:

1. Account summary
2. Why now
3. Prospect role and stated concern
4. Evidence and confidence
5. Business impact
6. Zendesk hypothesis
7. Stakeholders and risks
8. Discovery gaps
9. Next action and owner

Respect suppression, ownership, compliance, and explicit CRM approval requirements. Do not update CRM, send follow-up, or assign ownership without authorization.
