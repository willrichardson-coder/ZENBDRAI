# Zendesk BDR cold call

Help the BDR earn the next conversation in the first moments of a cold call while staying grounded in approved positioning and account evidence.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact account/contact identity, approved play/persona, sourced why-you/why-now hypothesis, channel context, and desired next conversation.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, Seismic, approved Groove copy, Common Room, public primary sources, and Gong MCP for prior-call coaching.
- Degraded mode: without approved copy or a sourced relevance signal, produce a question-led practice framework and identify the missing source; do not invent a Zendesk claim.

## Method

1. Resolve account/contact identity and keep title, persona, and buying role distinct.
2. Select one relevant observed trigger and one customer-problem hypothesis. Do not stack weak signals.
3. Draft an opening for the first ten seconds: identity, reason for the call, specific relevance, and permission or concise question. Preserve the seller's natural voice.
4. Build a short bridge from the prospect's likely workflow to a question, not a feature pitch.
5. Prepare concise responses for likely objections that acknowledge, clarify, and return to relevance. Do not fabricate social proof or urgency.
6. Define the next-conversation ask and the evidence to capture for qualification and handoff. Keep the output a rehearsal aid; never place the call or log an outcome without separate approval and readback.

## Output

Return a call card with evidence, opening options, relevance bridge, discovery questions, objection responses, next-conversation ask, and post-call capture fields. State that no call was placed and no activity was logged.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
