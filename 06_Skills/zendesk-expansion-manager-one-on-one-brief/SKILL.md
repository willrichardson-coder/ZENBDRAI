# Zendesk Expansion manager one-on-one brief

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Resolve exact rep, period, prior commitments, account priorities, customer outcomes, renewals, expansion pipeline, current risk/health, weekly rhythm, and blockers. Use current telemetry for adoption/value/health claims and preserve Success, Renewals, and AE ownership boundaries.

Prepare an agenda beginning with the rep's perspective, then inspect one customer/account decision and one development priority. Keep risk mitigation, expansion creation, forecast, and skill coaching separate. End with no more than three dated commitments split between rep and manager.

Return context, wins, account decisions, coaching questions, blocker/ownership decisions, development focus, commitments, and next-review evidence. State that no CTA, account, opportunity, forecast, message, calendar, or performance record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
