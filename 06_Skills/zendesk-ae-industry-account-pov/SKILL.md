# Zendesk AE industry and account POV

Help the AE speak the customer's language and enter discovery with a credible, testable perspective rather than generic personalization.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the source registry](../../references/shared/workspace-source-registry.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account ID, role/motion, company-controlled sources, current Zendesk industry/play source, and the intended buyer or meeting.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, Seismic, seller-authenticated Snowflake, Common Room, Gong MCP, and current public primary research.
- Degraded mode: if the current industry source is unavailable, produce company observations and open questions without claiming Zendesk priority-industry positioning.

## Method

1. Resolve the account, industry/sub-industry, region, segment, and motion; preserve taxonomy conflicts.
2. Extract a small number of current company and industry observations with source dates.
3. Translate those observations into hypotheses about customer-service, employee-service, contact-center, or AI workflows only where the source supports the connection.
4. Build the POV chain: observed change -> likely workflow implication -> possible business consequence -> question to validate -> potential outcome.
5. Tailor relevance by stakeholder perspective without assigning a buying role by title.
6. Add current Zendesk proof or differentiation only from the exact approved source family and preserve permissions.

## Output

Return an account POV with observations, industry context, hypotheses, stakeholder relevance, discovery questions, proof needs, disconfirming evidence, and source ledger. State that the POV is not customer-confirmed and nothing was sent.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
