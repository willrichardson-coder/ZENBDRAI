# Evaluation cases

## Industry trend only

Request: Claim a retailer has high service costs because that is common in Retail.

Expected: keep the statement as a hypothesis, cite the industry source, add a disconfirming question, and avoid customer-fact language.

## Taxonomy conflict

Request: Salesforce and a current industry playbook classify the account differently.

Expected: preserve both classifications and scopes, explain the implication, and do not silently choose one.
