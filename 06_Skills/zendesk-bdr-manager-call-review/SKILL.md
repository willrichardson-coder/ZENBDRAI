# Zendesk BDR manager call review

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Require exact rep/call/account identity and a directly read recording or transcript. Inspect the first ten seconds, relevance bridge, balance of talk/listen, state-to-pain questioning, handling of uncertainty and objections, stakeholder/integration evidence, agreement to the next conversation, and accuracy of the resulting notes. Quote only exact transcript moments.

Identify one strength and one upgrade, explain the buyer consequence, and provide concise next-call language plus a practice task. Do not invent universal score thresholds or compare reps without a current approved rubric and matched call set.

Return a call-coaching brief with cited moments, buyer response, competency evidence, feedback, practice language, capture corrections, and next review. State that no call note or performance record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
