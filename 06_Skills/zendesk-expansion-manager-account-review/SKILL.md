# Zendesk Expansion manager account review

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Core evidence is exact rep/account/opportunity IDs, review period, entitlement, renewal context, account-team ownership, current outcomes, and dated telemetry/health evidence. Without telemetry, do not judge adoption, value, health, or whitespace.

Inspect outcome attainment, adoption/consumption, implementation/support, relationship and executive coverage, live risk/CTA state, renewal timing, product/use-case whitespace, and expansion qualification. Keep renewal, risk mitigation, and expansion as distinct decisions. Success remains the front line for risk identification and next steps; the AE coordinates customer and commercial actions.

Return prioritized accounts, evidence and confidence, risks, whitespace hypotheses, owner gaps, manager interventions, rep commitments, and next review. State that no account, CTA, opportunity, renewal, or forecast was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
