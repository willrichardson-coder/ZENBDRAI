# Zendesk Customer Proof

Use when choosing a customer result for outbound, discovery preparation, a business case, or internal enablement.

## Approval test

A proof point is approved only when it has:

- Customer name
- Direct official Zendesk story URL
- Exact result or operational change
- Last-verified date
- Use-case and pressure match
- Approved wording that stays inside the source
- Any permission or reference restriction

Prefer the approved snapshot in `zendesk_customer_stories.md`. Treat the larger story archive as research leads unless each record passes the approval test.

## Workflow

1. Start with the prospect's operating pressure, not the industry.
2. Search approved customer proof for a matching consequence or outcome.
3. Verify the official story page and exact metric.
4. Write one concise, source-faithful sentence.
5. Label the record Approved, Reference lead, Restricted, or Retired.
6. If no proof passes, omit the metric and use a hypothesis or question instead.

Never invent, round, combine, or generalize customer results. Do not send or update CRM.
