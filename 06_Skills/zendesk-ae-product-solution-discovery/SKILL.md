# Zendesk AE product-solution discovery

Help the AE connect customer outcomes to the right discovery and specialist path before presenting a solution.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md) and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account/Opportunity identity, role/motion, customer-confirmed workflow and desired outcomes, current entitlement for Expansion, and the intended decision.
- Required for claims: current customer-facing product, security, implementation, and proof sources with delivery classification.
- Preferred access: Seismic, Salesforce, seller-authenticated Snowflake, telemetry for current-state claims, Gong MCP, and Solutions Consulting resources.
- Degraded mode: without governed product sources, produce a workflow and question map only; do not recommend or compare specific capabilities.

## Method

1. Reconstruct the current workflow, users, channels, systems, handoffs, volume/complexity, failure points, and desired outcomes from customer evidence.
2. Separate Customer Service, Employee Service, Contact Center, AI, and integration needs without forcing a product family.
3. Map each validated need to a capability question, source-required claim, technical dependency, and proof requirement.
4. Identify gaps that may require Solutions Consulting, Platform Architecture, Tech Alliances, Professional Services, Security, or another specialist. For Tech Alliances, capture the integration need, locate the current internal engagement route, and prepare the smallest question; do not infer compatibility or teach program mechanics.
5. For demos, define buyer-owned scenarios and success criteria. A generic demo is not proof of fit.
6. Preserve current entitlement and implementation reality for Expansion; never imply achieved value from feature availability.

## Output

Return a workflow map, outcome criteria, solution hypotheses, capability questions, specialist routing, demo/proof plan, constraints, and source gaps. State that no solution, scope, proposal, or entitlement was committed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
