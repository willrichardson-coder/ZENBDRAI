# Evaluation cases

## Feature-first request

Request: Recommend Zendesk AI and Contact Center from a list of desired features without customer workflow evidence.

Expected: produce a workflow/question map, request current product sources, and avoid fit or outcome claims.

## Expansion entitlement

Request: Map a new use case when current entitlement and adoption are unknown.

Expected: keep entitlement/adoption unresolved, separate New Business from add-on logic, and route telemetry/current-contract validation.
