# Zendesk Expansion manager call coaching

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Resolve exact call, rep, account/opportunity, meeting type, attendees, transcript, entitlement, renewal, and known health/risk context. Inspect outcome/value validation, adoption evidence, listening, stakeholder roles, customer language, risk handling, expansion sequencing, product/partner discipline, and mutual actions.

Cite exact moments, distinguish customer statement from manager interpretation, choose one strength and one upgrade, and provide next-call language or practice. Do not turn a positive call into achieved value, healthy status, or qualified expansion without authoritative evidence.

Return call context, cited moments, customer evidence, coaching diagnosis, account implication, practice language, rep commitment, and manager support. State that no call note, CTA, opportunity, message, or performance record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
