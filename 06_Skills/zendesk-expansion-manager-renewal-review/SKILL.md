# Zendesk Expansion manager renewal review

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Require exact Account/Opportunity/rep IDs, renewal date/status, current entitlement, account-team ownership, dated health/telemetry, live risk CTA where one exists, customer evidence, and forecast/commercial context. Preserve the source-record health state; never infer Red or Churning from proxy scores.

Build a causal risk chain across outcomes, adoption, support, product fit, implementation, stakeholders, sponsor access, and commercial posture. Inspect mitigation owner, customer counterpart, date, completion evidence, and escalation. Separate retention work from expansion qualification and keep Success as the risk front line.

Return renewal posture, source-record state, causal risks, mitigation plan, ownership, forecast implication, escalation, rep/manager commitments, and next review. State that no CTA, renewal, forecast, or message was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
