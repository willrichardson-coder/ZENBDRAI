# Zendesk Expansion value review prep

Prepare a customer-centered review that distinguishes realized value from internal assumptions and creates a useful next conversation.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the customer-risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md). Compose with the Sales plugin's meeting-prep and customer-quote skills when available.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account ID, review period, agreed outcomes/success measures, current entitlement, renewal timing, account-team ownership, and dated adoption/health evidence.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-callable Snowflake, telemetry/Customer Success health, Seismic, exact customer plan/MAP, and Gong MCP for prior-call evidence.
- Degraded mode: without telemetry, do not assert adoption, consumption, achieved value, or current health. Without Gong or supplied customer evidence, do not attribute language or commitments to the customer.

## Method

1. Resolve exact account identity, review window, motion, renewal context, customer attendees, and Zendesk owners. Keep AE, Success, Renewals, SC, and services responsibilities distinct.
2. Reconstruct the previously agreed outcomes and measures from attributable customer or mutual-plan evidence. A seller-entered objective is not customer agreement.
3. Compare intended outcomes with dated adoption, consumption, implementation, support, health, and value evidence. State metric definitions and windows; never convert product activity into business impact without an evidenced link.
4. Use live risk/health evidence where available. Apply the Seismic definitions only to the observed account state. Success is the front line for identifying risk and next steps; the AE coordinates rather than assuming Gainsight CTA ownership.
5. Map stakeholder participation, sponsorship, Economic Buyer access, Champion strength, and relationship gaps. Titles alone do not establish buying roles.
6. Select only current, delivery-appropriate product updates, customer proof, or benchmarks from the exact source family. Preserve permissions and never use the internal product comparison chart directly with a customer.
7. Build an agenda around customer validation: outcomes, evidence, gaps, decisions, and mutually owned next actions. Do not turn the review into an unvalidated expansion pitch.

## Output

Return a review brief with account/scope, outcome scorecard, adoption/value evidence, stakeholder map, risk and renewal context, customer questions, evidence-backed agenda, and mutual next actions. When the user requests a deck, use an available presentation capability after establishing this evidence brief; if none is available, return a slide-ready outline and clearly state that no deck file was created. The same evidence, claim, and ownership boundaries govern the presentation. Cite exact IDs/sources and state that no account, CTA, opportunity, message, customer plan, or presentation was changed or published.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
