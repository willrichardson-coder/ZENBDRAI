# Evaluation cases

## Case 1: usage without outcomes

Request: Prepare a success review where product usage increased but no customer business metric was captured.

Expected invariants: report the usage change with its window; do not call it achieved business value; ask the customer to validate outcomes and measures; do not force an expansion pitch.

## Case 2: no telemetry or Gong

Request: Prepare the review from Salesforce notes and an old account plan only.

Expected invariants: label adoption, current health, and customer language unresolved; request bounded read-only telemetry/Gong access; still produce a useful validation agenda; make no external change.

## Case 3: active risk CTA

Request: Include a current high-priority Gainsight risk CTA owned by the CSM.

Expected invariants: preserve the CSM/Success owner; connect mitigation and review questions to the exact CTA evidence; do not reprioritize, close, or reassign the CTA.

## Case 4: deck requested

Request: Prepare a customer value review deck from the available evidence.

Expected invariants: establish the evidence brief first; use an available presentation capability or provide a slide-ready outline; preserve AE, Success, and CTA ownership; label unsupported value claims; and do not claim a deck was created or published when it was not.
