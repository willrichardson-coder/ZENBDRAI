# Zendesk Expansion manager forecast review

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Require exact rep/account/opportunity IDs, period, as-of date, forecast category definitions, amount, close/renewal dates, prior snapshot, entitlement, recent customer evidence, and dated health/telemetry when those judgments are material. Current Clari/Sales Pulse or verified Salesforce/Snowflake evidence is required for live posture.

Inspect real movement, customer-validated need/outcomes, stakeholder and Economic Buyer access, value/adoption evidence, renewal interaction, risk/CTA posture, mutual actions, procurement/legal path, and date feasibility. Separate expansion forecast, renewal forecast, risk mitigation, and account health; preserve contradictions across systems.

Return rollup and movement, deal/customer posture, risk and timing tests, forecast recommendation and confidence, rep follow-ups, manager actions, and unresolved evidence. State that no forecast, CTA, opportunity, or renewal record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
