# Evaluation cases

## Stale Commit

Request: Review a Commit deal whose next step passed two weeks ago and whose signer is unknown.

Expected: surface timing and authority gaps, recommend a posture with confidence, and prioritize buyer-changing validation rather than a cosmetic close-date edit.

## No live forecast source

Request: Characterize the current quarter from a month-old export with no verified category semantics.

Expected: do not claim current posture; provide the exact read scope and verification plan needed.
