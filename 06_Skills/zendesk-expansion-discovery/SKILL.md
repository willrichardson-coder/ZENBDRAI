# Zendesk Expansion Discovery

Help the Expansion/CAM AE test a customer-value hypothesis before treating whitespace as a qualified opportunity.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the customer-risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md). Compose with the Sales plugin's meeting-prep and follow-up skills when available.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account/Opportunity IDs, current products/entitlement, renewal timing, account-team ownership, and the expansion hypothesis.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-callable Snowflake, telemetry/health, Seismic, customer plan/MAP, and Gong MCP when prior calls matter.
- Degraded mode: absent telemetry or call evidence, preserve adoption, health, and customer-language gaps and create questions to validate them.

## Method

1. Verify this is an Expansion motion. Route a genuinely new use case at an existing customer to New Business; route a straightforward automatic renewal to the truncated process.
2. Build a dated baseline of current outcomes, implementation, adoption/consumption, entitlement, support friction, health/risk, renewal status, and stakeholders. Separate customer-confirmed facts from internal whitespace models.
3. Frame one or more testable hypotheses linking observed customer change or unmet outcome to potential value. Product usage, unused entitlement, or modeled seats do not prove need, budget, or timing.
4. Prepare questions across current state, desired outcome, business impact, success measures, timing/compelling event, decision roles/process, Champion/Economic Buyer, technical fit, implementation readiness, renewal interaction, and alternatives/competition.
5. Apply Value Selling; apply Sandler only for SMB/Digital and BANT only where the current process requires it. Use MEDDPICC continuously without converting CRM completeness into evidence quality.
6. Use the current risk method where live evidence exists. Expansion must not bypass unresolved risk; Success remains the front-line risk owner.
7. Capture integration and implementation needs. Determine whether Tech Alliances, Professional Services, Solutions Consulting, or another specialist may need to be engaged, then prepare the smallest internal question using current guidance. Do not promise compatibility, scope, incentives, or terms or teach specialist-program mechanics.
8. Select current product, proof, and competitive sources by claim type and delivery classification. Treat AI synthesis as a locator and never use the internal product comparison chart as buyer-facing evidence.
9. Define the evidence that would qualify, reshape, defer, or stop the expansion pursuit. Draft follow-up only when requested; sending requires separate approval.

## Output

Return a discovery brief with motion check, evidence baseline, hypotheses, stakeholder map, prioritized questions, qualification/stop criteria, risk and renewal coordination, and proposed mutual next actions. Cite exact sources and state that no CRM, CTA, forecast, message, or customer plan was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
