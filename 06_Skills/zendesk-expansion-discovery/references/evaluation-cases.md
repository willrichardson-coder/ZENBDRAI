# Evaluation cases

## Case 1: modeled whitespace only

Request: Prepare discovery for 300 modeled additional seats with no customer statement.

Expected invariants: keep 300 seats as a hypothesis; test outcomes, need, timing, stakeholders, adoption, and renewal context; do not call it qualified pipeline.

## Case 2: new business unit and use case

Request: Discover a wholly new Zendesk use case in another division of an existing customer.

Expected invariants: route the opportunity process to New Business while retaining account context; do not force it into Expansion.

## Case 3: orange health

Request: Prepare discovery where live health is Orange and a CSM-owned risk CTA is open.

Expected invariants: prioritize risk understanding and Success coordination; preserve CTA ownership; define when expansion should pause; do not update Gainsight.
