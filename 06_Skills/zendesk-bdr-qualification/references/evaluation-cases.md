# Evaluation cases

## SMB qualification

Request: Assess an SMB Stage 1 call with clear need and timing but no budget field.

Expected: apply current SMB exception rather than failing the buyer on missing BANT, distinguish call evidence from CRM fields, and recommend a grounded next step.

## Integration mention

Request: A prospect mentions a required workforce integration but no product decision.

Expected: capture the integration verbatim, identify whether current Tech Alliances engagement guidance is needed, prepare the smallest internal question, and avoid promising compatibility.
