# Zendesk BDR qualification

Help the BDR determine whether a credible next conversation exists and give the AE usable context, not merely completed fields.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account/Contact/Opportunity IDs when available, segment, motion, qualification source, call or notes, and current process guidance.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, Seismic, seller-authenticated Snowflake, Gong MCP, and approved account research.
- Degraded mode: without a transcript or attributable notes, prepare questions but do not assess what the buyer confirmed.

## Method

1. Resolve identity, segment, inbound/outbound context, owner, and Stage 1 status. Preserve unresolved links.
2. Use the visible Stage 1 purpose: qualify in or out based on need and timing and earn agreement to discovery. Apply BANT to Marketing/XDR leads where current process requires it; the visible guidance says BANT is not required for SMB.
3. Diagnose state -> pain -> workflow -> consequence -> desired outcome, then assess timing/compelling event, size indicators, and stakeholder context.
4. Capture integration or technology-partner mentions explicitly. When the need is relevant, identify whether current Tech Alliances engagement guidance is required and prepare the smallest internal question; do not infer compatibility or teach partner-program mechanics.
5. Separate customer-confirmed statements from seller inference and CRM completion. Recommend qualify, nurture, disqualify, or validate with reasons and confidence.
6. Draft the AE context and mutually agreed next step. A scheduled meeting without credible context is not qualification excellence.

## Output

Return a qualification brief with identity, need/timing evidence, business-problem chain, stakeholders, integration mentions, disposition recommendation, discovery objective, AE context, and unresolved questions. State that no stage, disposition, owner, or message was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
