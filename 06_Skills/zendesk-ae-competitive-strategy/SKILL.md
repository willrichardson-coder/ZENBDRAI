# Zendesk AE competitive strategy

Help the AE change the buyer's decision criteria and proof plan using current Competitive Intelligence, not memorized claims.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md) and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md). Compose with the Sales plugin's competitive-brief capability when available.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account/Opportunity identity, role/motion, named or inferred competitor state, buyer decision criteria, stage, and current source date.
- Required for external assertions: current Klue/CI, PMM, product, security, legal, pricing, and proof sources appropriate to the claim.
- Preferred access: Seismic Competitive and Market Intelligence Hub, Gong MCP, Salesforce, seller-authenticated Snowflake, and customer correspondence.
- Degraded mode: without a current governed source, prepare discovery questions and a CI access request; do not generate a battlecard from memory.

## Method

1. Separate buyer-confirmed competitor, incumbent, internal build/no-decision, and seller inference.
2. Identify the customer's decision criteria, current preference, switching risk, and proof standard from attributable evidence.
3. Retrieve the exact current competitor and scenario source; preserve region, segment, product, quarter, and delivery restrictions.
4. Build differentiation as buyer-relevant outcomes and proof, not feature checklists or disparagement.
5. Prepare questions that expose tradeoffs, required evidence, traps to avoid, and a proof plan involving the right specialist.
6. State uncertainty and escalation needs for pricing, legal, security, roadmap, or unverified product comparisons.

## Output

Return competitive state, buyer criteria, source-backed differentiation, questions, proof plan, risks, do-not-say boundaries, and unresolved evidence. State that nothing was sent or recorded as buyer fact.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
