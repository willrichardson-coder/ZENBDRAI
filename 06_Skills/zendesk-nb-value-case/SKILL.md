# Zendesk New Business value case

Turn discovery evidence into a decision case that explains why change matters, why the proposed Zendesk path fits, and how value will be validated.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md). Use the Sales plugin's business-case capability when available.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account/Opportunity identity, customer-confirmed current state and consequences, desired outcomes, measures, stakeholders, timing, and decision context.
- Required for buyer-facing claims: current Seismic or an exact approved claim/proof ledger with delivery classification and source revision.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-authenticated Snowflake, Gong MCP, customer-provided metrics, product/solution sources, and the exact current customer story or reference.
- Degraded mode: when metrics or claim authority are missing, produce a value hypothesis and measurement plan, not calculated ROI or a send-ready business case.

## Method

1. Separate customer-confirmed facts, seller assumptions, benchmark inputs, and recommendations.
2. Build the causal chain from workflow problem to operational consequence, business impact, desired outcome, measure, and decision relevance.
3. Define a transparent baseline, calculation window, units, formulas, ranges, and sensitivity. Never present a midpoint or training example as customer value.
4. Connect only validated capabilities to the diagnosed workflow. Retrieve the current customer-facing product, security, implementation, competitive, and proof sources; never quote the internal product comparison chart.
5. Show who benefits, who owns each assumption, what evidence validates it, and what could make the case wrong.
6. Produce both an executive narrative and an auditable assumption table. Keep price, discount, procurement, and contractual authority outside the model unless current approved evidence is supplied.

## Output

Return an executive value narrative, problem-to-outcome map, quantified model or measurement plan, assumption ledger, capability/proof citations, stakeholder relevance, risks, and buyer validation questions. Label external status: draft only; no proposal, pricing, CRM, or customer message changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
