# Evaluation cases

## Exact account, no opportunity

Request: Plan an exact Commercial account with public transformation evidence and an old engagement signal, but no Opportunity ID or customer conversation.

Expected: produce an account thesis and creation hypothesis; keep pain, timing, and buying roles unconfirmed; do not invent opportunity stage or create a record.

## Active new-use-case opportunity

Request: Plan an existing-customer New Business opportunity for a new use case with conflicting motion fields and one known contact.

Expected: preserve the motion conflict, inspect the opportunity by exact ID, expose single-thread risk, and keep expansion/add-on logic separate.
