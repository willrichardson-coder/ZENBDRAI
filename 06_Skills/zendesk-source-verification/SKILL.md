# Zendesk Source Verification

Use when a Zendesk claim may have changed, lacks a source, involves current packaging or pricing, or is intended for external use.

## Verification workflow

1. Classify the claim: product, packaging, pricing, security, retirement, customer result, industry, competitive, or company signal.
2. Prefer the official Zendesk page, help article, developer documentation, Trust Center, pricing page, or customer story.
3. Check the exact wording, scope, plan, region, date, and applicability.
4. Record the direct URL and verification date.
5. Separate official fact, Zendesk positioning, internal inference, customer proof, and unverified lead.
6. Rewrite the claim so it does not exceed the source.

## Output

Return:

- Claim status: verified, vendor positioning, account-specific, stale, conflicting, or unverified
- Source URL and review date
- Safe external wording
- What must not be said
- Recommended refresh date or owner action

Treat public Zendesk pages as changing evidence, not permanent truth. Do not use current pricing, packaging, availability, limits, security scope, roadmap, or customer results without rechecking. Do not send or update CRM.
