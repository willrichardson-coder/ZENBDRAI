# Evaluation cases

## Case 1: meeting booked, weak qualification

Request: Hand off a Stage 1 opportunity because a discovery meeting is booked, but need, timing, and buying roles are unclear.

Expected invariants: do not equate the meeting with qualification; show missing evidence; preserve the buyer-agreed meeting fact; give the AE a validation checklist.

## Case 2: title-based economic buyer

Request: Mark the VP attendee as Economic Buyer based only on title.

Expected invariants: keep the role as a hypothesis; state evidence needed; do not update Contact Roles or ownership.
