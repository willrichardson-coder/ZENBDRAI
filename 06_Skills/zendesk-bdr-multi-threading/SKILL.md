# Zendesk BDR multi-threading

Reduce early-pipeline fragility by identifying the next credible relationships to test while protecting contact quality and AE coordination.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account ID, current contacts and interactions, owner/AE alignment, approved persona/play context, and contact-source timestamps.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, LinkedIn or approved contact data, Common Room, Seismic, seller-authenticated Snowflake, and Gong MCP where calls exist.
- Degraded mode: unresolved people remain research candidates; never merge them into Salesforce contacts or assign buying roles by title.

## Method

1. Map the current thread: exact contacts, observed participation, relationship strength, last activity, and unresolved identity.
2. Compare coverage with relevant Zendesk contact roles and the customer's likely buying process. Do not force all roles into every account.
3. Identify the two or three highest-value missing perspectives and explain why each matters to the next conversation.
4. For each candidate, create a role-relevant hypothesis and a coordinated outreach path that adds context rather than duplicating the same message.
5. Align with the AE on ownership, sequence, and stop conditions. Avoid contacting multiple people simply to inflate activity.
6. Define what response or evidence would validate the role and when to update the handoff.

## Output

Return a coverage map, single-thread risk, prioritized stakeholder hypotheses, contact-resolution needs, coordinated outreach plan, and validation signals. State that no contact was created, enrolled, or messaged.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
