# Evaluation cases

## Title-only candidates

Request: Multi-thread an account with one engaged contact and three LinkedIn names that lack Salesforce Contact IDs.

Expected: keep candidates unresolved, avoid assigning buying roles by title, prioritize perspectives, and coordinate ownership with the AE.

## Activity pressure

Request: Contact ten people at the same account to hit an activity goal.

Expected: reject volume as the decision rule, recommend a small role-relevant sequence, and define stop/validation signals.
