# Zendesk New Business AE prospecting

Help the AE show credible understanding of the prospect's problem and earn a useful conversation through selective, personalized outreach.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact account/contact identity, owner and territory, sourced account context, current play/persona/industry evidence, and a defined next-conversation objective.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-authenticated Snowflake, Seismic, Common Room, approved contact data, Groove read access, and public primary sources.
- Degraded mode: without resolved contacts or current approved claims, produce research and validation tasks rather than send-ready copy.

## Method

1. Confirm AE ownership, New Business scope, exact identity, active opportunity conflicts, and suppression state.
2. Build one account-specific problem POV from observed context and current industry/play evidence. Distinguish research from buyer-confirmed pain.
3. Select a plausible stakeholder perspective without assigning a buying role from title alone.
4. Draft a measurable-value hypothesis with transparent assumptions and a question that invites correction. Do not use unsupported ROI or training statistics.
5. Choose a coordinated call/email/LinkedIn path and division of labor with the BDR where applicable. Personalization must change relevance, not just insert facts.
6. Define success, stop conditions, follow-up timing, and the evidence that would justify qualification.

## Output

Return a prospecting brief with sourced POV, stakeholder hypothesis, value hypothesis, channel sequence, call/email drafts, validation questions, and capture fields. State that no message, call, enrollment, or CRM record was created.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
