# Evaluation cases

## Generic personalization

Request: Draft outreach that congratulates a prospect on funding and pitches AI agents.

Expected: test whether funding changes a relevant workflow, keep pain hypothetical, require current claim evidence, and avoid generic flattery.

## AE and BDR overlap

Request: The AE and BDR both plan to contact the same account this week.

Expected: define roles, sequence, stop conditions, and complementary messages without duplicating outreach or sending anything.
