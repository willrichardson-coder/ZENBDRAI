# Zendesk New Business discovery

Prepare the AE to diagnose the buyer's situation and earn a useful next decision. The plan should support a conversation, not script every sentence.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md). Compose with the Sales plugin's meeting-prep capability when available.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account and Opportunity IDs when present, meeting type, stage/motion/segment, attendees, objective, and current customer evidence.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, Seismic, calendar/email context, approved account research, and Gong MCP for prior recorded calls.
- Degraded mode: without prior-call access, do not attribute language, objections, commitments, or participation to the buyer. Prepare hypotheses and validation questions from the remaining evidence.

## Method

1. Resolve the discovery meeting, account, opportunity, participants, role, stage, and intended buyer decision. Keep unknown attendee identities visible.
2. Reconstruct prior customer statements and commitments only from directly read, attributable evidence.
3. Build the discovery chain: current state -> operational friction -> negative business consequence -> desired state -> measurable business outcome. Label every unsupported link as a hypothesis.
4. Select questions that test need, timing, success measures, decision process, stakeholder roles, competition, technical requirements, implementation, and next steps appropriate to the stage and segment. BANT is not required for SMB in the visible Stage 1 guidance.
5. When discovery exposes a later demo or workshop need, define the buyer-validated scenario and required success criteria. Route the actual meeting preparation to the general Sales meeting-prep capability and use current governed product/proof sources.
6. Plan opening, agenda, listening focus, role coverage, likely objections, and a buyer-owned close. Avoid interrogative question dumps and feature tours.

## Output

Return a meeting brief with objective, attendee map, evidence snapshot, problem/outcome hypotheses, prioritized questions, agenda, proof or specialist needs, likely risks, and the mutual next decision to seek. State that nothing was sent, scheduled, or written to CRM.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
