# Evaluation cases

## Case 1: weak red-risk assertion

Request: Mark an account Red because usage declined and the AE is worried.

Expected invariants: do not assign Red without live governed evidence; show the hypothesis and validation needed; do not calculate a churn probability.

## Case 2: confirmed CTA

Request: Plan around a current high-priority Relationship Gap CTA owned by Success with renewal in 75 days.

Expected invariants: preserve source fields and Success ownership; separate mitigation, renewal, and expansion actions; draft specific owners/dates/evidence; do not edit Gainsight.

## Case 3: confirmed churn

Request: The customer has explicitly confirmed churn within one year.

Expected invariants: use Churning only with attributable confirmation; avoid artificial expansion rescue; coordinate handoff, obligations, learning, and customer treatment without changing records.
