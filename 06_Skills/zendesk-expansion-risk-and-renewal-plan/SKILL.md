# Zendesk Expansion Risk And Renewal Plan

Create a customer-centered plan that clarifies risk evidence, ownership, mitigation, renewal interaction, and whether expansion should continue, pause, or be reframed.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the customer-risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact Account ID, renewal date/status, current account-team owners, and a dated live risk/health record or a clearly labeled risk hypothesis.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-callable Snowflake, Gainsight/CS health, product/support telemetry, Clari/Sales Pulse, Seismic, and Gong MCP for customer-call evidence.
- Degraded mode: without live risk/health access, structure a validation and coordination plan but do not assign a health state, CTA priority/status/reason, probability, or owner.

## Method

1. Resolve exact account, renewal, products/entitlement, commercial motion, and AE/Success/Renewals/SC ownership. Do not repair owner IDs by name.
2. Read the current health state and open risk CTA evidence with source date, reason, priority, status, due date, owner, summary, mitigation plan, and next update. Treat the visible Seismic reason list as incomplete until the live picklist is verified.
3. Apply the current Seismic definitions without inventing thresholds. `Red` includes a source-described 33-90% loss probability; do not calculate or assign that state from weak proxies. `Churning` requires confirmed churn.
4. Build a causal evidence chain across outcomes, adoption/consumption, support, product fit/capability, stakeholder engagement, sponsor/Economic Buyer access, implementation, renewal/commercial posture, and customer statements.
5. Keep risk resolution from becoming a performance judgment. Success is responsible for identifying risk and is the front line for next steps; the AE and Renewals coordinate around customer and commercial actions.
6. Evaluate renewal and expansion interactions separately. A mitigation action is not expansion qualification; active risk may require pausing, reframing, or sequencing expansion behind restored value and trust.
7. Draft a specific, customer-centered mitigation plan with actions, owners, dates, customer counterpart, and completion evidence. Preserve the current CTA record; any proposed update is an immutable draft requiring separate exact approval and write-path verification.
8. Evaluate a technology-partner or integration path only when it addresses a validated source of friction or value gap. Keep Success as the risk front line; if Tech Alliances involvement may be appropriate, identify the current internal engagement route and prepare the smallest question. Do not infer compatibility or use specialist involvement as a substitute for causal risk diagnosis.
9. Identify the executive escalation, specialist support, or customer conversation required and the evidence that would change the posture.

## Output

Return a risk/renewal brief with evidence snapshot, current source-record state, causal risk map, ownership, mitigation plan, renewal and expansion implications, escalation path, and next review point. State explicitly that no Gainsight CTA, Salesforce record, forecast, renewal, or message was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
