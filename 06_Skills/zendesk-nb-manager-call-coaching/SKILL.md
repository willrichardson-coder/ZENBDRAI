# Zendesk New Business manager call coaching

Read [the manager contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-manager-inspection-contract.md), [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), and [the risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md).

Resolve exact call, rep, account/opportunity, meeting type, stage, attendees, and transcript. Inspect agenda and positioning, current-state-to-outcome discovery, listening, pain/business impact, decision process, stakeholder roles, competition, value articulation, product/proof discipline, objections, mutual next step, and accuracy of CRM-ready notes.

Cite exact moments, explain the buyer consequence, choose one strength and one upgrade, and provide usable next-call language or practice. Keep deal intervention separate from rep development. Do not score against an invented rubric or claim buyer intent from tone alone.

Return call context, cited moments, buyer evidence, coaching diagnosis, practice language, deal follow-up, rep commitment, and manager support. State that no call note, CRM field, message, or performance record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
