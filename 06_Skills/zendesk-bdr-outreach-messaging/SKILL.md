# Zendesk BDR outreach messaging

Create concise, relevant outreach while preserving evaluated source copy, claim scope, and execution boundaries.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md), [the customer-risk and claims policy](../../references/shared/zendesk-risk-and-claims-policy.md), [the workspace source registry](../../references/shared/workspace-source-registry.md), and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md). Use the exact Sales Play/Groove source rather than inventing a generic Zendesk sequence.

## Access contract

- Core evidence: exact account/contact identity, approved play/persona context, current source copy or snippet, and one sourced `Why You, Why Now` angle.
- Required when claiming approved sequence language: current read-only Groove/Outreach source or exact revision-locked copy package.
- Degraded mode: without approved copy/claims, draft only a clearly labeled messaging hypothesis and validation questions; do not present it as approved outreach.

## Method

1. Verify exact account/contact IDs, role hypothesis, motion, segment, suppression/consent context, and source freshness.
2. Resolve the exact current play, persona overlay, sequence/snippet, claim ledger, product source, proof asset, and delivery classification needed. Do not blend plays or use blocked/pending claims.
3. Preserve evaluated wording, merge fields, links, threading, cadence, and channel constraints where the request is to reproduce approved copy. Do not silently improve or reconstruct missing source text.
4. Personalize only from sourced account/contact evidence. Never present public activity or modeled intent as customer-confirmed pain.
5. Use concise `Why You, Why Now` structure and a low-friction CTA for email or LinkedIn. For live-call openings or objection rehearsal, route to `zendesk-bdr-cold-call`. For video, keep the visible 30-60 second structure: intro, relevance/value, purpose, CTA, thanks.
6. Run a claim/source check and a recipient/context check. The internal product comparison chart and AI assistant synthesis are not buyer-facing evidence.
7. Return a draft plus source map and unresolved items. Sending, enrollment, activation, scheduling, or flow edits require separate immutable approval.

## Output

Return the exact draft, subject/threading where applicable, merge-field list, source/claim ledger, personalization evidence, and execution status. State `not enrolled / not activated / not scheduled / not sent`.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
