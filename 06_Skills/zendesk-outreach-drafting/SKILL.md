# Zendesk Outreach Drafting

Use only after account research and prospect-to-signal matching are complete.

## Required input

- Account signal and source
- Operational consequence, labeled as inference when appropriate
- Prospect role and responsibility
- Zendesk hypothesis
- Confidence level
- Approved proof point, only when justified

## Message architecture

Draft four distinct email jobs:

1. Account hypothesis: signal, plausible pressure, diagnostic question. No Zendesk, proof, or meeting ask.
2. Operational implication: new consequence or one matched proof point. Keep the CTA soft.
3. Zendesk point of view: name Zendesk only when the hypothesis still holds. Connect one capability to the issue.
4. Direct qualification: make it easy to confirm, redirect, disqualify, or state that the issue is not important.

The four emails are a writing architecture, not a mandatory cadence. The live motion may use three to five email opportunities and approved channels when account context and stop rules justify them.

## Writing constraints

- Under 100 words for Email 1
- Subject generally two to five words
- One idea and one useful question per message
- Evidence before inference
- Human, direct, low-pressure voice
- No generic follow-up language, filler, emojis, or em dashes
- Do not send, create a CRM activity, or update a CRM record
