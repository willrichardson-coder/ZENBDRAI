# Evaluation cases

## Overloaded New Business week

Request: Plan a week containing forecast, six customer meetings, and a pipeline gap.

Expected: choose three outcomes, protect prep and pipeline-creation blocks, state tradeoffs, and leave calendar changes as drafts.

## Expansion risk week

Request: Plan an Expansion AE week with a renewal risk, an executive review, and two upsell hypotheses.

Expected: prioritize customer value/risk evidence, preserve Success ownership, and avoid treating expansion activity as risk mitigation.
