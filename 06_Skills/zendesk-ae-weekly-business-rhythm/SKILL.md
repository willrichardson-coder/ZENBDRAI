# Zendesk AE weekly business rhythm

Help the AE own the week by protecting the work most likely to change buyer or customer outcomes while keeping operational commitments visible.

Read [the evidence/process contract](../../references/shared/zendesk-evidence-and-process.md) and [the tool-access contract](../../references/shared/seller-tool-access-contract.md).

## Access contract

- Core evidence: role/motion, week and timezone, current opportunities or customer book, stated priorities, fixed meetings, manager commitments, and known deadlines.
- Preferred access: Salesforce, seller-authenticated Snowflake, Clari/Sales Pulse, calendar, email, Gong MCP, and customer-health/telemetry for Expansion work.
- Degraded mode: without current system access, use a supplied dated snapshot and label the plan as provisional.

## Method

1. Resolve New Business, Expansion/CAM, or mixed scope; do not merge distinct pipeline, renewal, and customer-health definitions.
2. Identify the week's three highest-impact outcomes from current buyer/customer evidence, not activity volume alone.
3. Map immovable customer, internal, forecast, renewal, and follow-up commitments.
4. Protect blocks for pipeline creation, deal/customer advancement, preparation, follow-up, and system hygiene appropriate to the role.
5. State what will not be done, which risk that creates, and what new evidence would change the plan.
6. Add a midweek checkpoint and Friday readout based on observable outcomes. Calendar changes remain a draft until separately approved.

## Output

Return weekly outcomes, calendar-block proposal, prioritized account/deal/customer queue, dependencies, tradeoffs, checkpoints, and restart marker. State that no calendar, task, CRM, forecast, or customer record was changed.

For behavioral validation, use [evaluation cases](references/evaluation-cases.md).
