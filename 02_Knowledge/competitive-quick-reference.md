# Competitive Quick-Reference (Live-Call Cheat Sheet)

Fast glance during a call or while writing a subject line. For deep battlecards, pricing tables, objection scripts, and customer proof points, see `zendesk_competitive_intel.md` and knowledge base §7.

Default posture is **additive** - Zendesk complements these systems. Only shift to displacement framing when the account evidence or the prospect explicitly opens that door. Use [Zendesk Competitive Intelligence](zendesk_competitive_intel.md) for deeper context, and verify current claims before external use.

> **Evidence rule:** The incumbent descriptions below are conversation prompts, not neutral market facts. Do not state an exact competitor weakness, price, timeline, product limitation, or customer result without a current primary source. When uncertain, turn the statement into a discovery question.

| If they use / mention | Don't say | Do say |
|---|---|---|
| **Salesforce Service Cloud** | "Salesforce is bad" | "Keep it for sales if it works there. If service is becoming an operating bottleneck, how are you weighing native service depth, admin effort, AI usage, and the cost of the current stack?" |
| **ServiceNow** | "ServiceNow is bloated" | "It may be strong for IT service management. How are you handling external customer service, rollout speed, and the ownership between teams?" |
| **Freshworks / Freshdesk / Freshservice** | "Fresh is cheap" | "How are you evaluating scale, brands, channels, automation, and administration as the operation grows?" |
| **NICE / NICE CXone** | "NICE is legacy junk" | "Which parts of your current contact-center workflow require persistent case history, knowledge, or cross-channel ownership?" |
| **Genesys** | "Genesys is a mess" | "Where does the current contact-center architecture create the most operational or integration effort?" |
| **Avaya** | "Avaya is dying" | "When is the next meaningful contract or platform decision, and what would you want to improve before then?" |
| **Microsoft Dynamics 365** | "Dynamics is bad" | "Great generalist CRM. Native CX and AI depth is where it leaves gaps - that's usually where we come in additively." |
| **Oracle Siebel** | "Siebel is ancient" | "Modernize the service layer without touching the core CRM. Lower maintenance cost, better UX, no rip-and-replace." |
| **Jira Service Management** | "Jira isn't for this" | "Built for dev teams. Strong there. Not built for customer or employee service at scale - that's a different muscle." |
| **Eltropy** | "Eltropy is too niche" | "Solid for financial services specifically. Vertical-specific with channel limitations if you're scaling beyond that niche." |

---

**Rule of thumb for the call:** name the strength first, then the specific gap, then the additive frame. Never lead with the knock - it reads defensive and triggers a defend-the-incumbent reflex in the prospect.
