# Competitive Claim Verification Standard

## Purpose

Keep competitive guidance useful without turning seller positioning, vendor comparisons, or old pricing into unsupported market facts.

## Claim states

| State | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| Official vendor positioning | Published by Zendesk or the competitor | Attribute the claim to the vendor; do not present it as neutral truth |
| Primary-source fact | Current product documentation, pricing, filing, or announcement | Use with source URL and review date |
| Internal hypothesis | A question about incumbent cost, complexity, ownership, or fit | Use for discovery only |
| Unverified | No current primary source or conflicting evidence | Do not quote or use externally |

## LLM rule

Do not repeat exact competitor pricing, revenue, implementation timelines, TCO, staffing assumptions, analyst rankings, product availability, roadmap statements, or customer counts unless the record includes a current primary source and review date.

## Approved competitive motion

1. Acknowledge what the incumbent may do well.
2. Ask how the account measures the relevant operating outcome.
3. Test the gap with account evidence.
4. Position Zendesk only where the evidence supports a fit.
5. Avoid displacement language unless the prospect or account evidence creates a clear replacement case.
