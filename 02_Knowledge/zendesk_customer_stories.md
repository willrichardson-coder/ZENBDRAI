### Siemens Financial Services
**Region/Segment:** EMEA / ENT | **Industry:** Financial Services | **HQ:** Germany | **Employees:** 320K+

**Key Stats:**
- 86% CSAT
- +23% productivity increase (Nordics)
- ~2x productivity increase (France)
- 66% reduction in average resolution time (from 24 hrs to 8 hrs)

**Challenge:** Agents were managing customer interactions across shared email inboxes, spreadsheets, and paper trails - silos everywhere, slow resolution times, no visibility into ticket spikes.

**Zendesk Solutions:** Suite Enterprise (Support, Talk, Guide, Custom Objects, Sunshine Conversations, social messaging), Zendesk AI (Ultimate)

**Elevator Pitch:** Global B2B financing arm of Siemens adapted to a 30% spike in ticket volume by leveraging a custom Sunshine-based app and API - centralizing every customer interaction in one place and cutting resolution time by 66%, from 24 hours to around 8 hours.

**Story Link:** https://zendesk.com (search: Siemens Financial Services)
