## 1. WHAT ZENDESK IS (AND IS NOT)

Zendesk is an **AI-powered Resolution Platform** - not a ticketing tool, not a traditional helpdesk. The core positioning is: move beyond deflection to deliver real, end-to-end resolutions.

Everything runs on the **Resolution Learning Loop™** - every interaction makes the AI smarter over time. This is the key differentiator against static automation tools.

Default positioning is **additive, not replacement**. Zendesk complements existing infrastructure (Salesforce, ServiceNow, Microsoft, Oracle) rather than replacing it. This reduces objection surface and shortens the path to a discovery conversation. Only move to displacement framing when Will explicitly directs it.

Zendesk named a **Leader in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for CRM Customer Engagement Center.**

## 2. VALUE-FIRST SELLING FRAMEWORK

Will is a value-based seller. The prospect's pain always comes before Zendesk's product. Use this arc across every outreach sequence:

### The Four-Email Writing Architecture

**Email 1 - Make them feel understood**
- Signal + pain observation + curiosity CTA
- No vendor name. No proof point. No ask for a meeting.
- The prospect should think: "This person actually gets what I'm dealing with."
- 100 words maximum.

**Email 2 - New operational implication or useful proof**
- Add a new consequence in the prospect's area of responsibility
- When it genuinely helps, include one proof point matched to the pain and value delivered
- Still use a soft CTA
- Proof timing is a testable hypothesis, not a universal law

**Email 3 - Zendesk point of view**
- Name Zendesk only when the account hypothesis still holds
- Full value prop tied to their specific pain
- Use one customer story with a hard stat only when it is matched to their situation
- Ask for the conversation directly but with low friction

**Email 4 - Direct qualification**
- Make it easy to say the issue is relevant, owned elsewhere, already covered, or not a priority
- End the sequence cleanly without manufactured urgency

This architecture builds from account context to operating relevance, then tests whether Zendesk belongs in the conversation. It is our operating standard, not a claim that four emails will outperform every other cadence.

## 4. FULL PRODUCT MAP

### CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE (CX)

**Support (Core Ticketing Engine)**
Central data-processing and workflow-orchestration engine. Ingests raw incoming events from connected channels and normalizes them into structured, trackable ticket objects. Native IT Asset Management (ITAM) tracks physical and software assets (500 baseline, expandable). Advanced Data Privacy & Protection (ADPP) framework with French Health Data Hosting (HDS) certification for protected health information.
*Pain it solves: multi-channel ticket chaos, asset visibility gaps, data governance and compliance exposure*

**AI Agents**
AI agents automate customer and employee requests, including complex workflows and backend actions, using connected knowledge, context, and business rules. Zendesk's current public packaging no longer uses Essential versus Advanced as the default distinction. Verify channel availability, plan inclusion, resolution allowances, and account-specific pricing against the current official AI Agents documentation.
*Pain it solves: high ticket volume, agent burnout, 24/7 coverage gaps, scaling without headcount, complex multi-step resolution*

**Agent Copilot**
Proactive AI assistant embedded in every agent's workflow. Surfaces full customer context before agents respond. Ticket summarization, writing assistance, real-time call suggestions. Suggests replies, adjusts tone, executes agent-approved actions. Pulls from full knowledge base, solved tickets, and business procedures. Available at no cost for Suite Professional plans and above. Increases team productivity by 20%+.
*Pain it solves: long handle times, inconsistent agent quality, high AHT, slow onboarding ramp, incomplete customer view during calls*

**Voice AI / Contact Center (Talk / Zendesk Voice)**
Native VoIP, PSTN, and digital embedded lines. International toll-free numbers, local lines, direct extensions, targeted blocklists. Unified voice + digital in one system. IVR infrastructure: keypad-tone routing to voicemail, agent groups, external numbers, nested menus, or SMS deflection. Voicemail transcription (15 seconds, 35 languages). Idle-time or omnichannel routing based on agent status and skills. Eliminates hold music and scripts. Handles authentication and routine resolutions naturally on calls. Replaces fragmented legacy stacks. Enterprise-grade without middleware complexity.
*Pain it solves: high call volume, long hold times, legacy telephony tech debt, fragmented channels, voicemail chaos*

**Intelligent Triage & Advanced Routing**
Reads intent, sentiment, language, and topic automatically at ingestion. Routes to the right agent or team without manual sorting. Intent quality recommendations flag duplicate or overlapping intents for administrative refinement.
*Pain it solves: misrouted tickets, manual sorting burden, slow first response times, routing conflicts*

**Quality Assurance (QA - formerly Klaus)**
Auto-scores 100% of AI and human interactions without human sampling. Outlier detection flags sentiment deviations, escalation risks, customer churn signals, and "dead air" on voice. Automatic group synchronization updates agent groups from Support Admin Center. Structured coaching workflows with pinned conversations/metrics, documentation, performance goals, and knowledge quizzes.
*Pain it solves: inconsistent service quality, compliance risk, agent performance blind spots, inability to review at scale, coaching overhead*

**Workforce Management (WFM - formerly Tymeshift)**
AI-powered predictive forecasting, automatic agent scheduling, schedule adherence tracking, out-of-adherence automation. Combined workstreams view, granular time-off reasons by team, 24-hour activity visibility and average handle time (AHT) tracking baseline (included in Suite Professional+). Reduces overstaffing and understaffing. Integrates with agent performance and ticket volume data.
*Pain it solves: scheduling inefficiency, over/understaffing costs, manual schedule management, activity/adherence blind spots*

**Omnichannel Agent Workspace**
Single unified workspace covering email, chat, voice, social, messaging, and side conversations (Slack/Teams). Full customer context, persistent conversation history, sidebar extensibility via Zendesk Apps Framework (ZAF). Context panel shows customer profile and related records.
*Pain it solves: channel fragmentation, agents toggling between tools, incomplete customer view, context switching friction*

**Guide (Self-Service & Knowledge Management)**
Knowledge base and self-service engine. Hierarchical content (Categories, Sections, Articles) with user segments for granular permissions. Native web crawler (Zendesk/External-Content user-agent, adaptive for client-side JS, default 4-level depth, 20 concurrent crawlers/instance limit). Automatic link discovery for complex sitemaps. Federated search: connects to external repositories (SharePoint, Notion, Guru, Google Drive, Box, Amazon S3, Document360) - indexed for search in help center, Agent Workspace context panels, and AI agent responses. Unified article editor. Official Bangla language support added.
*Pain it solves: outdated self-service content, agent knowledge gaps, slow article creation, external knowledge silos*

**Reporting & Analytics (Explore)**
Real-time and historical reporting across all channels (replaced legacy batch reporting). 250+ OOTB pre-built dashboards tracking FRT, CSAT, agent utilization, SLA compliance, channel volumes. Show empty rows toggle for inactive queues, 30-day historical trend extension, brand-level permissions for agent-group restricted metrics. Expanded Light Agent permissions: grant report-management or revoke analytics access entirely.
*Pain it solves: lack of CX visibility, siloed data, inability to tie service data to business outcomes, reporting complexity*

### PLATFORM & INTEGRATIONS

**Custom Objects (Relational Framework)**
Native relational custom-objects framework - replacing deprecated Sunshine Custom Objects (retired July 27, 2026). Administrators define fields as columns, records as rows. Supports lookup relationships (custom-to-custom, custom-to-standard: tickets, users, organizations). No nested JSON or hierarchical arrays. 100 custom fields max per object, 32KB per record. Platform capacity: 50M custom object records/account, API responses limited to 100 records/page via cursor pagination. One-to-one relationships managed via external validation logic (not natively enforced).
*Pain it solves: complex data modeling, relational data silos, schema migration from legacy systems*

**Integration / Action Layer**
1,800+ apps and integrations (marketplace). 1,300+ pre-built apps, 600+ public APIs. Native connections: Shopify, Salesforce, Workday, Slack, Jira, Microsoft Teams, Asana, Zoom. Zendesk Integration Services (ZIS): event-driven hosted web service for multi-step workflows (ZIS Flows, ZIS Actions, ZIS Connections, ZIS Playground app). No-code flow builder. APIs, webhooks, SDKs. Action Credits: usage-based custom-action execution (plan-specific limits). Pre-built Custom Object Templates (Contracts, Orders, Products, Projects, Subscriptions).
*Pain it solves: disconnected tech stack, manual data entry, no single source of truth, workflow automation gaps*

**Zendesk Apps Framework (ZAF) & ZCLI**
Developers build custom apps running inside sandboxed iframes in Agent Workspace. ZCLI: create, validate, test, package locally before production deployment. ZAF standardized JavaScript SDK for cross-frame communication. Interface constraints: sidebar apps ≤320px, navigation bar apps full height/width, modals up to 80% viewport. Authentication via browser cookies (internal) or secure parameters (external). OAuth refresh token flows required (password-based parameters deprecated).
*Pain it solves: custom workspace requirements, integration gaps, rapid feature customization without full development cycles*

**Security & Compliance**
Enterprise-grade encryption, global data hosting. GDPR, CCPA, regional data residency compliant. Granular permissions, full audit logs, automated data deletion. French Health Data Hosting (HDS) certification for protected health information (PHI). Advanced Data Privacy & Protection (ADPP) add-on ($50/agent/mo): additional security controls and compliance frameworks.
*Pain it solves: data privacy risk, compliance requirements, lack of governance, PHI/regulated-data exposure*

## 9. PROSPECT PRIORITY RANKING

### Tier 1 - Highest Priority
Economic buyers. Own the budget and the pain.
- Chief Customer Officer, Chief Experience Officer, Chief Information Officer, Chief HR Officer
- VP / SVP / EVP of Customer Experience, Customer Service, Support, Contact Center
- VP / SVP of IT, HR / People Operations
- VP of AI / Digital Transformation

### Tier 2 - Strong Priority
Director-level. Own the day-to-day pain. Often drive or heavily influence the buy.
- Director of Customer Experience / Service / Support / Contact Center
- Director of IT / IT Operations / ITSM
- Director of HR Operations / Employee Experience
- Director of AI / Automation / Digital Transformation
- Director of Operations / CX Operations

### Tier 3 - Supporting Priority
Multi-threading targets. Not primary outreach unless Tier 1 and 2 are exhausted.
- Senior Manager of CX / IT / HR / Operations
- Head of [CX / IT / HR / Support]

**Manager-level exception - check authority before applying Skip/Flag:**
"Manager" and "Senior Manager" carry different real weight at different companies, and title alone is not a reliable signal. Before skipping a Manager-level contact, check their actual described scope (LinkedIn bio, role description) for:
1. **Delegated vendor/tool evaluation or governance authority** - e.g. a bio that says they "vet third-party tools," "own governance," or "assess enterprise fit" for a system in Zendesk's pain categories (CX/IT/HR/AI). If present, treat as Tier 1/2 regardless of title - this person has real buying-process influence a title alone wouldn't suggest.
2. **Access to or influence with senior leadership relevant to a Zendesk pain** - e.g. an HR Business Partner who advises senior leadership on org design. Keep as Tier 3 multi-thread even without direct buying authority; useful for account multi-threading and evidence-led routing.
3. **Whether their functional scope intersects a Zendesk-relevant pain category at all** (CX/IT/HR/AI/Ops per §10's persona map). If their scope is unrelated (e.g. a Continuous Improvement manager in industrial engineering, with no tie to service/support systems), the manager-level nuance does not apply - treat as Skip/Flag regardless of company size.

*(Real case, 2026-07-11, VF Corporation: Benjamin Scheuer, title "Senior Program Manager, AI," bio explicitly states he vets third-party GenAI tools and owns governance for enterprise fit - despite title, ranked Tier 1/2, not skipped. Contrast Traci Otsuji, "Manager, Continuous Improvement Programs" - no tie to CX/IT/HR/AI, correctly stays low-priority.)*

### Skip / Flag
- Manager level and below (unless Will directs otherwise, or the Manager-level exception above applies)
- Titles outside CX, EX, IT, HR, AI, Operations, Procurement
- 🔴 FLAG: Any title where the Zendesk angle is unclear
