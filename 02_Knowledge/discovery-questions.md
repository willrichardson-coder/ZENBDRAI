# Discovery Call Question Bank - MEDDPICC

**Use after a call is booked.** These questions surface MEDDPICC gaps on the discovery call itself. Weave three to four into a conversation; never interrogate. Ask, listen for the gap, and prescribe the next step.

---

## Identify Pain (lead here)

- "Walk me through what happens when a customer reaches out today. Where does it get stuck?"
- "When volume spikes, what breaks first - response time, agent capacity, or quality?"
- "How much of your team's day goes to questions a good self-service layer could answer?"
- "What's your cost per ticket right now, and where do you want it?"
- (EX) "When an employee files an IT or HR request, how do they track it? Or do they just email and hope?"

## Metrics (quantify the pain)

- "What's your current CSAT, and what would moving it 5 points be worth?"
- "How many tickets per month, and what's the trend quarter over quarter?"
- "What does one point of first-contact resolution improvement mean for headcount?"
- "If you automated 40% of inbound, what would that free your team to do?"

## Economic Buyer

- "Who owns the budget for customer experience tooling - you, or does it sit higher up?"
- "When you've bought platforms like this before, who had to sign off?"

## Decision Criteria (set competitor traps here)

Match the trap to the incumbent identified in Phase 1 research (`zendesk_competitive_intel.md` §4 / knowledge base §7).

- "If you evaluated something new, what would it have to do that your current setup can't?"
- "How important is native AI versus bolting automation onto an existing tool?" (**Salesforce** trap - CRM-first, not service-first, expensive add-ons for voice/messaging/AI)
- "How fast do you need to be live - weeks or quarters?" (**ServiceNow** trap - 12-18 month implementations)
- "What happens to your model when volume doubles?" (**Freshworks/Freshdesk** trap - lacks enterprise-grade AI/WFM/QA at scale)
- "How's the telephony piece holding up as you grow?" (**NICE** trap - no native ticketing, two separate WEM products)
- "Any concerns about vendor stability with your current contact center provider?" (**Avaya** trap - aging infrastructure, financial instability)
- "Is IT stretched thin maintaining what you've got?" (**Genesys** trap - heavy IT lift, long sales cycles)

## Decision Process & Paper Process

- "Who else would weigh in - IT, procurement, security?"
- "What does your procurement cycle usually look like start to finish?"
- "Any contract renewals on existing tools coming up?" (displacement timing - cross-reference the ICP signal in knowledge base §11)

## Champion & Competition

- "Who internally feels this pain most? Who'd push hardest for a fix?"
- "Are you looking at other options, or is 'do nothing for now' on the table too?"

---

## 30-Second Self-Intro (for cold calls / events)

> "Will Richardson with Zendesk. I work with [vertical] teams handling [pain] without hiring linearly. Is that on your radar, or are you in a good spot there?"

Keep it under 15 seconds of talking, then ask and listen. Use this only for cold calls or event conversations when that channel is appropriate.

---

*After the call: record the outcome in the approved CRM or account record. If no system is available, capture the decision, evidence, owner, and next action in the account worksheet.*
