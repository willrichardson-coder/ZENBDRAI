# Enterprise BDR Operating Controls

## Purpose

Define the controls around research and drafting so enterprise BDR work is reviewable, compliant, measurable, and handoff-ready.

This is an operating checklist, not legal advice. Apply the requirements of the relevant jurisdiction, company policy, sending platform, and approved compliance owner.

## 1. Before research

- Confirm account ownership and territory.
- Confirm the account is in scope and not excluded.
- Confirm the approved research sources.
- Confirm whether the account is cold, warm, active, customer, partner, or existing opportunity.
- Check current suppression, opt-out, do-not-contact, and legal restrictions.
- Record the research date.

## 2. Before drafting

- Complete the account signal map.
- Keep verified facts, inferences, and unknowns separate.
- Select one primary signal per prospect.
- Confirm the prospect's current role and business connection.
- Confirm the Zendesk capability is relevant to the signal.
- Confirm any proof point is Approved under [Customer Proof Verification Standard](customer-proof-verification-standard.md).
- Confirm any competitive claim is permitted under [Competitive Claim Verification Standard](competitive-claim-verification-standard.md).

## 3. Before sending

No draft is a send authorization. A human owner must confirm:

- Recipient, account, territory, and owner.
- Sender identity and approved mailbox.
- Compliance basis and opt-out process.
- Suppression and prior-contact status.
- Source links for material claims.
- Sequence stop rules.
- Final copy and channel.

Do not send, create an external message, or update a CRM record from a draft unless the authorized owner has explicitly approved that action.

## 4. CRM and account-record minimum fields

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Account | Name, domain, segment, territory, owner |
| Research date | Date and researcher |
| Account status | Cold, warm, active customer, opportunity, partner, excluded |
| Primary signal | Verified fact and source URL |
| Inference | Plausible operating implication, clearly labeled |
| Prospect | Name, title, function, source URL, confidence |
| Outreach angle | One signal-to-role connection |
| Proof | Story, source URL, review date, approval state |
| Sequence state | Not started, drafted, approved, active, replied, stopped |
| Outcome | Positive reply, neutral reply, objection, meeting, opportunity, opt-out, bounce, no response |
| Next action | Owner and date |

## 5. Stop and suppression rules

Stop the motion when the prospect:

- Requests no further contact.
- Opts out or enters a suppression state.
- Replies with a clear disqualification.
- Changes roles or leaves the company.
- Is already owned by another seller or active opportunity.
- Shows a material risk that cannot be supported by approved evidence.

Record the reason and next owner. Do not add a touch merely to finish a sequence.

## 6. AE or account-team handoff

A handoff is ready only when it contains:

- The account trigger or operating pressure.
- Evidence and source links.
- The prospect's stated issue or response.
- Impact, metric, or consequence if known.
- Current environment or incumbent if verified.
- Stakeholders and decision role.
- What Zendesk capability is relevant and why.
- Open questions.
- Agreed next step, owner, and date.

Do not hand off a meeting as qualified based only on seniority, a positive sentiment, or a calendar acceptance.

## 7. Measurement

Track outcomes by account, segment, persona, signal, channel, and sequence state:

- Positive reply rate
- Qualified conversation rate
- Meeting acceptance rate
- Meeting-held rate
- Opportunity creation rate
- Opportunity progression
- Opt-out and unwanted-contact rate
- Bounce and deliverability failures
- Time from research to approved draft
- Time from reply to human response
- Handoff acceptance rate
- Handoff-to-opportunity conversion

Do not use opens as a primary success metric. Treat response patterns as evidence to test, not proof that one phrase caused an outcome.

## 8. Review cadence

- Review active suppression and ownership before each new account motion.
- Review deliverability and unwanted-contact signals weekly.
- Review positive replies and qualified conversations monthly.
- Review proof, product, pricing, and competitive sources whenever Zendesk changes the source page.
- Update rules only after reviewing a meaningful sample and recording the evidence in the Change Log.
