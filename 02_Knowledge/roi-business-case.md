# ROI / Business-Case Frames

Use when a prospect shows interest and you need to convert curiosity into a booked call, or when a discovery call needs a business-impact close. These frames extend the repository's value-first standard: connect a relevant operating issue to cost-to-serve, retention, or revenue when the evidence supports it.

---

## The cost-per-ticket story

- Industry baseline: a human-handled ticket runs ~$3-$5 fully loaded.
- An AI-resolved interaction costs a fraction of that.
- If AI Agents deflect 40-80% of routine volume, the savings compound monthly.

**Frame:** "At [X] tickets a month, even a 40% deflection rate changes your cost structure materially. That's the conversation worth having with [AE]."

## The consolidation story (kills the budget objection)

- Tool consolidation is a discovery hypothesis, not a default outcome. Confirm the prospect's current chatbot, QA, WFM, telephony, admin, and contract costs before estimating savings.
- Do not use the 42% TCO claim or any generic 2-3-tool retirement claim without the dated primary source and account-specific cost model.

**Frame:** "I'm not asking for new budget. Teams your size are consolidating their stack and freeing OpEx. Worth having the blueprint for next year's planning."

## The headcount-avoidance story

- Growth usually means hiring more agents linearly with volume.
- AI breaks that line - handle more without proportional headcount.

**Frame:** "If volume doubles next year, do you want to double the team? Here's how others broke that curve." *(Pair with a proof point from knowledge base §5/§6 matched to their pain - e.g. HealthJoy: 2x support capacity, -25% ticket volume.)*

---

## Discovery-to-meeting bridge (prescriptive close)

Use only when the prospect has shown interest or at the end of a discovery call. Do not use this bridge in the first touch. Follow the CTA rules in the [Email Outreach Drafting Standard](../01_Rules/email-outreach-drafting-standard.md).

> "Given you're [evaluating X / feeling Y pain], the efficient next step is to put your numbers against what we're seeing in [their vertical]. Do you have 15 minutes Tuesday with [AE] and me?"

---

## Notes

- Every stat used here must trace exactly to [Customer Stories](zendesk_customer_stories.md), [Zendesk Product Map](zendesk_expertise_knowledge_base_v3.md), or a newly verified source. Do not invent numbers.
- Right-size the pitch: if the account's ticket volume is low, lead with the consolidation/visibility angle instead of the volume-dependent deflection math - don't oversell a hook where the numbers don't land.
