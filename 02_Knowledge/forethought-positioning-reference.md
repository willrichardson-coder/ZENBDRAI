# Forethought Positioning Reference

## Purpose

Use this reference when positioning Forethought capabilities in Zendesk sales work. It records learnings from the Forethought Resource Center reviewed on 2026-09-09. It is enablement context, not current product approval or account evidence.

## Positioning themes

- Forethought extends beyond support deflection into voice, proactive engagement, lead qualification, pre-sales, product recommendations, onboarding, retention, renewal reminders, internal sales enablement, legal intake, and sensitive account workflows.
- Orchestrator can trigger workflows from events, schedules, APIs, webhooks, spreadsheets, sentiment changes, CSAT, and ticket aging.
- Autoflows can use natural-language instructions, conditions, loops, forms, authentication, MFA, API calls, browser actions, and backend updates.
- Discover identifies contact drivers, knowledge gaps, failed deflection, and workflow opportunities. Solve resolves inquiries. Triage classifies and routes. Assist guides agents. Agent QA evaluates closed tickets.
- A useful business frame is moving from answering questions to completing workflows, while improving the system from interaction data.

## Additional use cases

- Voice password resets and pricing questions.
- Voice-guided onboarding and scheduled renewal or event reminders.
- Proactive outreach after service failures.
- Lead qualification and routing by account value or fit.
- Sign-up assistance, product discovery, cross-sell, and retention or cancellation flows.
- OCR-supported troubleshooting, legal and safety intake, and identity-verified sensitive changes.
- Slack-based internal sales and marketing knowledge retrieval.
