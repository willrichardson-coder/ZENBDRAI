## Coverage standard

The repository is complete for a BDR topic only when the relevant entry has:

1. An official source URL.
2. A last-verified date.
3. A concise explanation of the product or business relevance.
4. Approved language for external use, if applicable.
5. A separation between official fact, internal positioning, inference, and proof point.
6. An owner or review action when the source changes.
