# Zendesk Official Product Facts

**Status:** Canonical official-fact layer for BDR use.

**Last checked:** 2026-08-25

**Use:** Treat these as source-grounded descriptions of Zendesk's public positioning. Verify pricing, packaging, limits, availability, customer results, and roadmap claims again before using them externally.

## Core positioning

Zendesk presents itself as an AI-powered service platform and Resolution Platform. Its public positioning emphasizes real resolutions across customer and employee service, not only ticket tracking or deflection.

Official sources:

## Voice, messaging, and analytics

Zendesk Messaging supports real-time and asynchronous conversations across web, mobile, and social channels. Zendesk positions it around a unified Agent Workspace, omnichannel routing, AI agents, business workflows, and analytics. The official Messaging page also describes deploying one AI agent across multiple messaging channels and migrating legacy live-chat experiences toward Messaging.

Sunshine Conversations extends messaging through a unified API for custom bots, external applications, reporting tools, web and mobile messaging, and third-party channels. Current Suite Professional, Enterprise, and Enterprise Plus documentation describes a low-volume self-service version through Admin Center. Channel availability, usage, subscription terms, and integrations remain account- and product-specific.

**Current transition rule:** Legacy Sunshine Conversations customers may be moved from the legacy dashboard to Zendesk Admin Center or may have the legacy account shut down on February 1, 2027. Zendesk says the affected account receives an email with the applicable path and effective date. Do not present the date as a universal migration deadline for every Sunshine Conversations customer.

BDR relevance:

- Use Messaging when the account needs continuous, cross-channel customer or employee conversations without forcing agents to switch tools.
- Use Sunshine Conversations when the account has custom bots, external channels, backend integrations, or API-driven messaging workflows.
- For an existing Sunshine customer, first establish whether the account has received a migration or shutdown notice, then ask about integrations, keys, dependencies, and operational risk.

## Marketplace, partners, and implementation support

Zendesk Marketplace organizes the ecosystem into apps, connectors, themes, and partners. Current categories include AI and bots, agent productivity, contact center, analytics, CRM and marketing, ecommerce and payments, messaging, workflows, security and compliance, WEM, knowledge, IT, and HR. Treat Marketplace listings, availability, pricing, and partner claims as current-page facts that require verification.

Zendesk's partner program describes four broad motions: resell, refer, implement, and technology. The ecosystem also includes technology partners, solution providers, business process outsourcers, and global strategic partners. Professional Services covers launch, optimization, AI Services, training, and ongoing expert support.

BDR relevance:

- Marketplace and partner evidence can explain how Zendesk fits an existing stack without requiring a rip-and-replace conversation.
- Professional Services is relevant when the account has deployment risk, limited internal expertise, complex integrations, or an AI implementation concern.
- Do not promise a specific partner, implementation duration, certification, or deployment result without current source and account fit.

- Encryption and data hosting options
- Compliance and certification information
- Data protection and privacy controls
- Data deletion and sensitive-data redaction
- Granular permissions and audit logs
- AI governance and quality controls

Source: [Zendesk Trust Center](https://www.zendesk.com/trust-center/)

The Trust Center is the authoritative source for current security and compliance claims. It currently presents evidence and artifacts covering SOC 2 Type II, ISO certifications, AI governance, FedRAMP LI-SaaS, industry programs such as PCI-DSS, HIPAA, and HDS, data residency, encryption, SSO, audit logs, and privacy controls. Certification scope, product applicability, report access, and regional availability must be confirmed before external use.
