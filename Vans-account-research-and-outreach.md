## Account signal map

### Signal 1: Vans is in a recovery phase with different channel trends

- Verified fact: In VF's Q1 FY27 results, Vans revenue was down 8% year over year, with continued growth in Americas DTC more than offset by global wholesale declines. VF expects Vans wholesale to improve significantly in the second half of the fiscal year. [VF Q1 FY27 earnings](https://www.vfc.com/investors/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000103379-26-000113/q12027earningsexhibit991.htm)
- Reasonable inference: The operating question is not simply whether demand is improving. It may be whether teams can see customer and partner signals consistently across DTC, wholesale, regions, and handoffs while the channel mix changes.
- Possible consequence: Different teams may work from partial views of customer issues, partner requests, product feedback, or delivery friction.
- Functions and roles: Strategy and transformation, digital experience, customer experience, commercial operations, wholesale operations, digital innovation.
- Zendesk fit hypothesis: A shared service operating view, routing, knowledge, and reporting across channels, without assuming a replacement of commerce or wholesale systems.
- Confidence: High for the business signal. Medium for the service consequence.

### Signal 2: VF is using a digital-led, social-first growth model

- Verified fact: VF says it shifted toward a more social-first, digital-led marketing strategy and that understanding consumer demand has been central to product and consumer-relevant experience decisions. [VF FY26 Form 10-K](https://www.vfc.com/investors/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000103379-26-000030/vfc-20260328.htm)
- Reasonable inference: Digital experience and loyalty teams may need a clearer way to connect customer interactions to recurring friction, campaign response, and product or experience decisions.
- Possible consequence: Feedback can remain fragmented across digital channels, service teams, social activity, and product or marketing workflows.
- Functions and roles: Loyalty, CRM, digital experience, consumer insights, marketing technology, product, digital innovation.
- Zendesk fit hypothesis: Messaging, knowledge, workflow, AI-assisted service, and service analytics that turn interaction patterns into usable operating signals.
- Confidence: High for the digital-led strategy. Medium for the service-data gap.

### Signal 3: The operating model is being simplified while product and technology work is consolidated

- Verified fact: VF says it consolidated product into a single global engine, elevated design to improve innovation and speed to market, upgraded inventory planning with AI-enabled capabilities, and focused on organizational alignment and simplification of Digital and Technology. [VF FY26 Form 10-K](https://www.vfc.com/investors/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000103379-26-000030/vfc-20260328.htm)
- Reasonable inference: Transformation leaders may be balancing central standards with brand and regional execution, which can expose unclear ownership and slower handoffs.
- Possible consequence: Requests and feedback may be difficult to classify, route, and measure across global functions and local teams.
- Functions and roles: Enterprise transformation, digital technology, innovation, product, design, operations.
- Zendesk fit hypothesis: Structured request workflows, knowledge, routing, and reporting for customer or employee service, integrated with the existing technology estate.
- Confidence: High for the operating-model change. Low to medium for the specific request-management gap.

### Signal 4: Cost discipline remains part of the growth plan

- Verified fact: VF continues to focus on margin expansion, SG&A reduction, debt reduction, and reinvestment in profitable growth. [VF FY26 Form 10-K](https://www.vfc.com/investors/financial-information/sec-filings/content/0000103379-26-000030/vfc-20260328.htm)
- Reasonable inference: Any service or workflow investment will need to show clearer visibility, lower manual effort, or better use of existing teams. This is an inference, not a stated Vans service priority.
- Possible consequence: Teams may be more interested in targeted workflow improvement than a broad platform replacement.
- Functions and roles: Strategy, transformation, digital technology, customer operations, finance-linked operating leaders.
- Zendesk fit hypothesis: A focused service layer that can organize work, automate repeatable requests, and measure outcomes alongside existing systems.
- Confidence: High for the cost context. Low to medium for the service implication.
