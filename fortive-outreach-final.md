# Fortive research and prospect outreach

## Account research

### Verified signals

1. Fortive completed the separation of Ralliant on June 28, 2025. Fortive now reports Intelligent Operating Solutions and Advanced Healthcare Solutions as its two segments.
2. Fortive describes a focused operating model built around the Fortive Business System, with more than 10,000 employees in approximately 50 countries.
3. Fortive's 2025 10-K says FBS has been infused with AI capabilities and that the company is using AI and machine learning to improve growth, productivity, and impact.
4. Fortive's people strategy includes global performance and development systems, skills-based learning, and quarterly employee feedback touchpoints.
5. Intelex is a Fortive Intelligent Operating Systems company. Fortive says Intelex provides digital EHSQ workflows and serves more than 75 Fortune 500 companies.

### Working inferences

- A post-separation operating model may increase the value of consistent internal service intake, ownership, and reporting across functions. This is a hypothesis, not evidence that Fortive has a service-management gap.
- An AI-focused operating system may create pressure to connect service interactions, recurring requests, and workflow signals to improvement decisions. This is a hypothesis, not evidence that Fortive or Intelex lacks that visibility.
- Global people and talent programs may create recurring employee support demand across channels and teams. This is a hypothesis, not evidence that Fortive's current process is manual or fragmented.

### Zendesk fit hypotheses

- IT and infrastructure: Employee Service, ITSM, intelligent triage, and reporting.
- HR and talent: HR Case Management, self-service, routing, and reporting.
- Product and SaaS: unified customer interaction data, recurring-theme detection, and routing to product owners.

## Final audit

- 7 prospects enumerated, with 4 emails each.
- Email 1 is product-free and proof-free for every prospect.
- All Email 1 bodies are under 100 words.
- Subjects are 2 to 5 words.
- Each prospect has one primary angle.
- Each email uses one main idea and one reply-focused question.
- No meeting ask, generic follow-up language, emojis, or em dashes.
- Fortive facts are separated from inferences in the research section.
- Zendesk is positioned as an additive service layer, not a replacement for core systems.
- Proof points appear only in Email 2 or Email 3 and are matched to the prospect angle.
- No email was sent and no CRM record was changed.
